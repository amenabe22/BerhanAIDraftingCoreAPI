## Q1
A. Commercial in nature. Under Article 1(1) of the Commercial Code (Proclamation No. 1243/2021), business organizations are defined as associations established for the purpose of carrying out commercial activities.

## Q2

## Q3

## Q4
A. Unless otherwise agreed needs unanimous consent to transfer them to outsiders. Under Article 462(1) of the Commercial Code (Proclamation No. 1243/2021), shares in a private limited company are freely transferable among members, but their transfer to non-members requires the consent of the majority of members representing at least three-fourths of the capital, unless the articles of association provide for a higher majority or unanimity.
