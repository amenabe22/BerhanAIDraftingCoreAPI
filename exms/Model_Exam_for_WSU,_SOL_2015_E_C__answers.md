## Q21
D. All. According to Article 38(1) of the Federal Income Tax Proclamation No. 979/2008, the Proclamation applies to residents of Ethiopia with respect to their worldwide income. Article 38(2) further states that it applies to non-residents with respect to their Ethiopian source income. This means that residence matters for foreign source income for residents, and source becomes important for non-residents. Additionally, Ethiopian source income is subject to tax regardless of the residence of the person generating such income.

## Q22
D. All. According to Article 3(3) of the Federal Income Tax Proclamation No. 979/2008, income derived by a non-resident that is attributable to a business carried on through a permanent establishment in Ethiopia is taxable. This includes income from the sale of goods in Ethiopia and rental income from immovable property located in Ethiopia if it is attributable to such a permanent establishment. Additionally, Article 31(1) states that a non-resident body conducting business in Ethiopia through a permanent establishment is liable for tax on repatriated profit.

D. The Ethiopian income tax proclamation relinquishes its jurisdiction when an international agreement to which Ethiopia is a party gives such jurisdiction to another state. This indicates that Ethiopia respects international agreements in matters of tax jurisdiction.
